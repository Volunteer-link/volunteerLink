    Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to develop the Vietnam Volunteer Link (VTL). Vietnam Volunteer Link aims to help connect volunteers with organizations seeking support for various social and community initiatives. The platform includes features to discover opportunities, manage volunteer activities, and facilitate communication between volunteers and organizations. We want to make it easy and effective for organizations to connect with enthusiastic people who are willing to donate their time and skills, and for volunteers to find worthwhile opportunities.

    Product Background
VolunteerLink was created in response to the growing need for a user-friendly, effective platform that links volunteers with charitable organizations. Nowadays, a lack of a centralized system, ineffective communication, and restricted reach make it difficult for many non-profit groups to attract qualified volunteers for their projects. However, those who want to volunteer frequently struggle to find opportunities that fit their availability, interests, and skill set.

Conventional volunteer recruiting techniques, such email lists, word-of-mouth, and ads, take a lot of time and don't take advantage of contemporary technologies to expedite the process. This results in a mismatch between supply and demand: while eager volunteers are not aware of opportunities that fit their preferences and capabilities, some organizations are unable to recruit enough volunteers.

    Solution
The platform will bring organizations and volunteers together by allowing organizations to post events and volunteers can easily sign up for events that match their skills and interests.
It will provide to users features such as schedule volunteers, track attendance, provide feedback, and suggest events that fits volunteers profiles.
Through the mix of communication and event management tools, the software will decrease operational stress for organizations while also ensuring that volunteers have a clear and enjoyable experience.
This solution will not only increase the visibility and accessibility of volunteer opportunities, but will also support the long-term goal of increasing community participation through technology-driven solutions.
    
    Opportunity
There is a strong market demand for a streamlined platform that connects organizations and volunteers. Missing integrated solutions is causing many organizations to struggle with managing and coordinating volunteer activity.
Existing platforms are either entirely focused on fundraising or offer limited help for volunteer management. This generates a need for a complete system that not only connects organizations and volunteers, but also improves overall activity coordination.
As more people, especially high school students, university students, and businesses look to take part in social impact activities, a platform that simplifies volunteer involvement and improves transparency becomes more appealing.
The trend of technological change in the non-profit sector boosts the adoption of such platforms, offering a long-term and expanding market opportunity.
